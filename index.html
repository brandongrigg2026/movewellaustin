Frequently asked questions

What to expect

Have a question not listed here? Call Rebecca or text Rebecca

Where does the training take place?

Move Well Austin comes to you. Rebecca provides one-on-one personal training in your home, making it easy to exercise in a comfortable, familiar environment without traveling to a gym.

Do I need prior exercise experience?

No. Sessions are adapted to your starting point, whether you are new to exercise or returning after time away.

Is this appropriate for adults over 50?

Yes. Move Well Austin is specifically focused on adults 50+ and on safe, progressive coaching for strength, balance, mobility, and confidence.

What should I wear?

Wear comfortable clothing that allows you to move freely and supportive athletic shoes unless Rebecca recommends otherwise for a specific session.

How often should I train?

Frequency depends on your goals, schedule, recovery, and current activity level. Rebecca will discuss an appropriate plan during your consultation.

Can you work around physical limitations?

Programs can often be modified around limitations. Share relevant information during your consultation, and obtain medical clearance when your healthcare provider recommends it.
